Docryvia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026. Applies to Docryvia Android v0.9 release candidate.

Publisher: choicematrix
Privacy and support contact: help@choicematrix.in
Public policy: https://subha760.github.io/lootchaser-privacy/docryvia-privacy.html

Data stays on your device Docryvia is an offline password and document vault. It has no developer-managed account, cloud sync, internet permission, advertising or analytics service in the release build. choicematrix does not receive your vault contents, master password, recovery answers, recovery codes or biometric templates through the app. No vault information is sold or used for advertising.

You choose what to store: passwords, usernames, website addresses, authenticator seeds, password history, custom fields, notes, address/identity/travel records and imported files. Files are stored separately with authenticated encryption and random identifiers; their names and vault metadata remain encrypted. These records and their metadata are encrypted in app-private storage. The app decrypts them while you use an unlocked vault. AES-256-GCM protects vault data; PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations protects password-based key wrapping. Encryption does not protect information already disclosed to another app, a compromised device, or an untrusted keyboard.

Unlocking and recovery The master password stays on the device and is not sent to choicematrix. Optional quick unlock uses Android's biometric/device-credential interface and Keystore. Android controls fingerprint, face and screen-lock authentication; Docryvia does not store biometric templates.

New vault setup requires a question and answer. Question recovery stores a question identifier and an encrypted key envelope. It requires both your answer and a separate random rescue code. Neither the answer nor that rescue code is stored in plaintext. Successful question recovery consumes the kit. Main recovery-key reset also invalidates the question kit. Password changes and resets disable the app's stored device-unlock wrapper; you can enable it again afterward. The main recovery key remains valid until you rotate it.

An unlocked session stays in RAM for up to 30 seconds after the app's activities leave the foreground, allowing brief file-picker/browser switches without another login. Screen-off, manual lock, timeout and process death clear that session. Foreground inactivity locking defaults to two minutes and can be set to one, two or five minutes, or disabled while open. A Quick Settings tile can manually lock the vault. Sensitive screens use protected display windows, subject to device/OS behavior.

Permissions and browser autofill The release build requests biometric authentication permissions and lets you enable its Android AutofillService in system settings. Camera permission is optional and requested only when you launch the offline QR scanner to add an authenticator. QR decoding runs on-device; no images or seeds are sent to a server. The app can query launcher applications so you can explicitly associate a login with an installed app. It does not request internet, SMS, contacts, location, microphone, broad storage, overlay or accessibility permissions. File access uses Android's picker for files you select.

Android may supply browser form metadata and entered values to the local autofill service. Docryvia checks exact HTTPS hostnames for supported browsers. Filling requires authentication or an already authenticated session, and your explicit account selection. Saving requires your approval. Only the chosen login is returned to the destination. Native-app filling requires your explicit association and a match with the saved app-signing identity. Browser fields embedded in native apps are not treated as verified native fields. Supported keyboards may show inline suggestions, with dropdown fallback. Browser/native-app support is limited and is not guaranteed for every form.

Exports, clipboard and sharing An encrypted ZIP backup is created only at a destination you choose and uses its own passphrase. An encrypted temporary export may be staged in app-private no-backup storage while a picker is open. It is removed on completion/cancellation; abandoned files older than 24 hours are cleaned at a later app startup.

Supported Google, 1Password and KeePass CSV and unencrypted Bitwarden JSON imports are plaintext source files. Docryvia previews them locally and encrypts accepted records after your confirmation; you must remove unwanted original export copies yourself. TOTP codes are generated offline from encrypted seeds using your device clock. Passwords and EFF-wordlist passphrases are generated locally using SecureRandom. PDF and image previews use private RAM/shared memory and do not create a plaintext preview file. An explicitly requested document export still creates a decrypted copy at your selected destination. Address sharing requires your confirmation and Android's share sheet. Explicitly requested credential, generated-password and recovery-key clipboard copies are marked sensitive where supported and scheduled for clearing after 30 seconds only if the clipboard still contains that particular app-owned copy; OS behavior and copies taken by another app are outside Docryvia's control.

Once you share, export or autofill information, the destination, operating system, file provider, browser and other apps follow their own policies. Docryvia cannot revoke or remotely delete their copies. Opening this public policy launches a browser, which contacts GitHub Pages under GitHub's privacy policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Retention and deletion Local records remain until you delete them or clear/uninstall the app. Deleting an item moves it to encrypted Trash, excluded from normal views and autofill. You can restore it or permanently delete it. Entries older than 30 days are purged at the next unlock. Up to five previous passwords are retained in an entry's encrypted history. Permanently deleting an entry removes its history and referenced encrypted document from the current vault. Encrypted backups include Trash and history; an older backup can retain records deleted later. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app's local vault and access material. Android backup/data-transfer settings are disabled for the vault; keep your own encrypted backups. Exported files and external copies remain until you remove them. Physical flash-storage remnants and historical backups may persist; secure physical erasure is not guaranteed.

There is no remote Docryvia account to delete. You can review, edit, export or delete your records locally. If all usable passwords/recovery methods are lost, choicematrix cannot recover the encrypted vault for you.

Support and changes If you email support, the email and information you voluntarily send are handled by the email provider and choicematrix to address your request. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or identity documents. Contact help@choicematrix.in for privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.

This is a release candidate and has not received an independent security certification. Review device testing and maintain encrypted backups before relying on it for valuable data. If data handling changes, the effective date and this policy will be updated. New cloud, analytics or permission-dependent features require an updated policy describing their behavior.
