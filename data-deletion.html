Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Lootchaser respects your privacy and your right to control your personal information. This page explains how you can request deletion of personal data associated with your use of Lootchaser services or interactions with our Meta/Facebook integration.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of your personal data, please send a request to the following email address:

Email: lootchaser2026@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your request, where applicable:

  • Your name or username, if applicable.
  • The email address associated with your request.
  • Your Facebook or Meta account information, if the request relates to our Meta integration.
  • A brief description of the data you would like deleted.

Processing Your Request

After receiving your request, we will review it and take reasonable steps to verify the request when necessary. We will process eligible data deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws and our Privacy Policy.

If we determine that we hold personal information covered by your request, we will delete or anonymize it where required, unless we are legally required or permitted to retain it.

Meta and Facebook Data

If you have interacted with Lootchaser through Facebook or other Meta services and would like to request deletion of information associated with that interaction, please contact us using the email address above.

Please note that Meta may independently retain certain information according to its own policies. For information about data held directly by Meta, you may also need to use Meta's own privacy and account data controls.

Data Deletion Confirmation

Once your request has been reviewed and completed, we will provide confirmation where appropriate using the contact information provided in your request.
